Engineering: process design and independent engineering review

We verify the design before it is approved.

We design processes from concept to technical definition, and review designs and vendor packages independently. We turn the technical question into a model that can be examined, and close the study with a conclusion the decision can rest on.

What we offer

Conceptual process designEarly feasibility and decision support: block flow diagram (BFD), preliminary PFD, design basis and assumptions.
Basic process designPre-FEED technical definition: final PFD, stream tables, and an equipment list with its sizing basis.
P&ID development and reviewNew or corrective P&IDs and control-intent inputs, for design clarity and lower risk.
Independent review and owner's engineerDesign verification and risk review of PFDs, P&IDs, simulations and vendor packages, closing with a written technical opinion; owner's engineer support by stage.

What you receive

  • A documented design basis and assumptions
  • Reviewable mass and energy balances and models
  • Comparison across scenarios and operating cases
  • Diagnosis of constraints and sources of performance loss
  • Recommendations ranked by impact, risk and feasibility
  • An independent technical opinion for approval or redesign

Sectors: chemicals and petrochemicals · power generation · water treatment and desalination · energy storage and batteries · cooling and thermal systems · food and pharmaceutical industries. Engagements do not include vendor datasheets, detailed mechanical design or EPC deliverables unless stated in the scope.

Starting point: a design before you approve it, or a system whose performance you doubt.
