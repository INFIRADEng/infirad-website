  • Works on your libraryYour documents, definitions and methodology, not only general knowledge.
  • Documents every outputInputs, actions and references are logged for the specialist to review.
  • Defined permissionsEach agent has a clear scope and sends nothing outside the organization without human approval.

How does workflow automation differ from an AI agent?

Automation executes defined steps and rules. An agent suits tasks requiring interpretation and controlled use of sources and tools. The task determines the approach; a solution may combine automation and agents with human review points.

How does a company start a specialized AI agent project?

Choose a recurring task and identify inputs, outputs, sources, systems and examples of correct work and critical cases. Start with a limited scope and clear acceptance criteria, measuring quality before expansion.

How are agent permissions and company data requirements defined?

Sources, permissions, deployment environment, confidentiality and human review requirements are defined before integration. Details depend on the organization's systems and project scope. The service description is not a compliance certification or comprehensive security guarantee.
