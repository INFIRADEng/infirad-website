How we build an agent

  1. Step 1
    Understand the work

    We map inputs, steps and decision points, and find where effort repeats.

  2. Step 2
    Build and connect

    We turn your method into an executable flow and connect it to your sources and systems.

  3. Step 3
    Test and measure

    We test normal and critical cases, and measure output quality before any expansion.

  4. Step 4
    Run and improve

    We monitor performance and the decision log, and improve the agent from real use.
