We verify the design before it is approved.

What we offer

Basic process designPre-FEED technical definition: final PFD, stream tables, and an equipment list with its sizing basis.

We test the idea before capital is committed.

We model processes and systems to measure performance and cost, compare alternatives, and size equipment on a documented basis, from mass and energy balances to 3D flow and heat transfer. The value of simulation is not the picture. It is the decision you can prove.

What we offer

Steady-state process simulationMass and energy balances, flow diagram development, and equipment sizing.
Optimization and heat integrationParametric and sensitivity analysis, trade-off comparison, and heat recovery by pinch analysis.
Computational fluid dynamics (CFD)Flow and turbulence, pressure drop, heat transfer, and mixing of Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids.
Thermal and dynamic modeling0D, 1D and 3D thermal modeling of batteries, electronics and cooling systems; transient behavior and control-system interaction.

What you receive

  • A documented model you can review and re-run
  • Alternatives compared on the same measures
  • Where the constraint sits and what causes performance loss
  • A recommendation memo for the next step

Public-sector engagement

INSYAB

A traffic decision, tested before it is built

Traffic decisions are usually tested after construction, when reversing is expensive. INSYAB models the network and calibrates it to observed traffic behavior, then simulates the proposed change, measures its effect across the whole network, and compares alternatives on the same measures.

  • Intersection and corridor upgrades
  • Bottleneck diagnosis
  • Closures and diversions
  • Impact of new developments
